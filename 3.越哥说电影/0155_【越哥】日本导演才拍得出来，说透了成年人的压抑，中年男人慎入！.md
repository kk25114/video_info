# 【越哥】日本导演才拍得出来，说透了成年人的压抑，中年男人慎入！

## 简介

中年职员龙平因公司业务迁往中国而被裁，隐瞒失业每日假装上班，结识同样失业的黑须并学习伪装技巧。经济压力与自尊受挫让他与妻子、两个儿子矛盾加深：长子欲参军离家，幼子偷偷学琴，黑须自杀、妻子遭劫持、龙平车祸等接连冲击这个家庭。经历混乱一夜后，一家人重新聚首，选择与生活妥协，继续前行。

With a Chinese greeting, is it even the book reality like her? Ryuhei never imagined that after joining the company right after graduation and finally reaching a section manager position, his job would be snatched away by a Chinese person. Because the company wanted to expand its overseas operations, Ryuhei's department was being moved to China, and high-salaried Japanese employees like him were heartlessly discarded by management, fired on the spot. After packing his bags, Ryuhei sat dazed in the plaza downstairs, surrounded by others who, like him, had just lost their jobs. Ryuhei didn't even have time to drop off his luggage before rushing to the employment office, but there was already a long line, and the daily quota for applications was full, so he had to give up on job hunting for the day and head home dejectedly. To hide the fact that he was unemployed, he threw everything he had brought from the office into the trash can. Since it wasn't time to get off work yet, he tried to sneak in through the back door, but his wife caught him, though luckily she wasn't suspicious and just assumed he had left work early. To maintain the lie, Ryuhei got dressed and left early the next morning, pretending that he was still going to work. A moment later, he joined the crowd of commuters, but he had no idea where he was actually supposed to go.

Ryuhei had 2 sons. The eldest, Takashi, was in university, attending classes during the day and working part-time at night to help with tuition. Leaving early and returning late, he had less and less communication with his family. The youngest, Kenji, was still in elementary school and had recently become obsessed with the piano, lingering to watch whenever he passed the music room after school. He had no idea that his father, currently facing a jobless crisis, no longer had any spare money to pay for piano lessons.

Meanwhile, Ryuhei submitted his resume to the employment agency, but the jobs they matched him with were either low-paying security guard roles or shady nightclub management positions; none of them suited him. And then, age became Ryuhei's biggest obstacle. His so-called work experience didn't help him; instead, it became a burden. For the suitable positions, crowds of young people were fighting tooth and nail, leaving no room for him. That evening at the dinner table, Kenji mentioned wanting to learn the piano, but Ryuhei rejected him without a second thought, dismissing it as just a fleeting impulse. Looking at his disappointed son, Ryuhei couldn't find the words to explain, forced to get by with such a clumsy excuse.

The next day at noon, Ryuhei, wandering outside, went to collect a free relief lunch. In the long line, he was the only one wearing a crisp suit, making him stand out. He unexpectedly ran into his former classmate, Kurosu, there. Seeing his sharp attire, Ryuhei felt a bit envious, never imagining he was also there for relief food. Watching Kurosu wolf down his food, Ryuhei was surprised by Kurosu's honesty; in fact, Kurosu was worse off than he was, as it had been 3 months since he was let go by his company. He still hadn't found a suitable job. He still wore a suit and carried a briefcase every day, occasionally pretending to take calls from clients in front of others. The 2 men, both stuck in a life crisis, smiled at each other and decided to get through these tough times together. Kurosu taught Ryuhei many survival tricks for the unemployed, like how bookstores are the best place to kill time—you can stay all day without spending a dime—or setting an alarm on your phone to ring periodically so you can pretend to take a call and look busy. As for how to hide it from his wife, Kurosu was also very experienced; he suggested Ryuhei put his severance pay into a separate bank account, as she would likely discover it if left in his original one. These practical tips made Ryuhei feel enlightened.

However, Kurosu's family life had also been tense lately; because he kept coming home early, his wife became suspicious, so he asked Ryuhei to pose as a colleague and come home for dinner, as the saying goes, “If you're going to act, see it through.” During dinner, Kurosu didn't forget to pretend to go out to take a call, and his wife took the opportunity to ask Ryuhei about the company; it was clear Kurosu's recent strange behavior had made her suspicious, so Ryuhei had to carefully reassure her. Hearing that the company was doing well, his wife finally felt relieved. Unexpectedly, once Kurosu returned, he insisted on overacting, putting on an air of authority and criticizing Ryuhei for not working hard enough. This completely baffled Ryuhei, who could only lower his head and apologize.

Early the next morning, he went out for an interview as usual. The interviewer was much younger than him and said, “I want to see what skills you can contribute to this company.” Caught off guard, Ryuhei immediately explained that he had worked at headquarters his whole life and possessed strong interpersonal skills, but this clearly failed to convince the other party. After being severely humiliated, Ryuhei met up with Kurosu again. He vented his anger at a nearby pile of trash, but Kurosu seemed to have long since become indifferent to everything. In his view, their situation was like a sinking ship with water already up to their necks; they knew there was no hope, yet they still struggled in desperate death throes. After saying this, he walked into the crowd of fellow unemployed people, leaving Ryuhei standing there alone, lost and bewildered.

Although his father did not approve of Kenji learning the piano, Kenji secretly took the lunch money his mother gave him and signed up for 1 month of piano lessons. That day, the teacher suddenly received a phone call, and Kenji inadvertently discovered that the teacher was going through a marital crisis and an impending divorce, and Kenji unexpectedly said he could understand the teacher's feelings. The teacher didn't take a child's words to heart, telling him to finish practicing and go home to study immediately.

On the other side, Takashi suddenly confessed something to his mother: he was applying to join the U.S. military, had already passed the test, and could enlist once he passed the physical exam; he hoped she would sign her consent, and she knew she couldn't stop him. She had no choice but to agree, but the matter still needed to be discussed properly with his father.

At the same time, Ryuhei received bad news: Kurosu, who usually seemed so calm and composed, had actually committed suicide. He and his wife died of gas poisoning, though fortunately, their daughter was unharmed. This event was a huge blow to Ryuhei; he was terrified that Kurosu's fate would befall him as well. That evening, Takashi brought the documents for him to sign to consent to his enlistment, and Ryuhei naturally refused. No parent wants their child to risk their life joining the military, let alone traveling all the way to America to do so. He didn't realize that his son's desire to join the military was just an excuse; at his core, he simply wanted to escape this home. Then a remark from Takashi left Ryuhei completely speechless, as the words cut deep into him.

The next day, he set aside his pride and found a cleaning job at a shopping mall far from home, while Takashi made up his mind to head to America. Before leaving, he asked his mother why she didn't divorce his father; the question caught her completely off guard. Although life was getting worse by the day, she had never once considered divorce, so she was stunned that it was her son who brought it up first. Although she stated that she didn't mind her role as a mother in the home, Takashi's words had planted a seed in her heart.

Just as the issue with her son was settled, a new problem arose with her younger son. Because 3 months of piano fees were overdue, Kenji's teacher contacted his mother, and that was how she found out he had been secretly taking piano lessons. However, she didn't blame Kenji and even intended to let him continue his lessons. Originally, the mother and son planned to keep it from Ryuhei, but that same evening, Ryuhei received another letter from the piano teacher. It turned out the teacher thought Kenji was very talented and should be trained for art school, so he wrote to persuade the couple. Upon finding out, Ryuhei flew into a rage. He didn't care if his son was a genius; he just wanted to establish authority at home. Perhaps after losing his former social status, he was venting his sense of control onto his son. The reason she never mentioned it was out of consideration for Ryuhei's pride; she kept silent simply to protect his ego.

Furious and desperate, Ryuhei vented all his anger on his son and accidentally pushed Kenji down the stairs. The couple immediately rushed the child to the hospital. While they were waiting outside the ward, the television was broadcasting news of the war, reporting that all new recruits drafted from Japan had been sent to the front lines, including their son, Takashi. This added insult to injury for the already strained couple. Fortunately, Kenji only had a mild concussion and was in no serious danger. The turmoil temporarily came to an end, but the cracks in everyone's hearts continued to grow.

One day, while cleaning the restroom, Ryuhei accidentally found a bag of money. He hesitated for a moment before stuffing it into his pocket, planning to slip away, but unexpectedly ran into his wife at the mall. In this moment, Ryuhei... As for why his wife suddenly appeared at this mall, we must go back to 3 hours earlier. A robber suddenly broke into Ryuhei's home, but they had no cash in the house. At that moment, a siren sounded outside. Fearing she would call the police, the robber forced her to flee with him. The 2 wandered aimlessly through the streets in a stolen car. Actually, this robber was also an ordinary person driven to the brink by life. He used to be a locksmith, but he had no business sense. He couldn't get his business off the ground, so he eventually just used his lock-picking skills to turn to theft—it's just that he didn't seem to have any talent for being a thief either.

The wife... Knowing the robber was also a pathetic man, she planned to use the excuse of going to the restroom to buy him some food at the mall. She never expected to run into Ryuhei there. Watching Ryuhei's panicked back as he fled, she became completely hopeless. She returned to the robber... The wife... She drove all the way to the seaside, and the 2 stood on the pier. After speaking, she charged straight toward the sea, but was grabbed from behind by the robber. 2 people despairing over life were left with nothing but helplessness. The robber dragged Ryuhei's wife into a small seaside cabin; the long-lost intimacy made her... together. In reality, this robber represents a group of typical Japanese people. Their nature isn't bad, but they can't adapt to modern society and are forced into crime. Yet, they remain kind-hearted, unable to even commit to being criminals, ultimately ending up with nothing.

That night, Ryuhei's wife was drawn to the starlight outside. When she reached the shore, the light that seemed to signify hope vanished, and the sea returned to total darkness, much like her own life. She sat by the beach for a long time that night, finally drifting off to sleep in the shallow water. On the other side, Ryuhei kept running until nightfall. Exhausted, he collapsed into a pile of trash.

The Japanese workplace: the work environment is under extreme pressure. Many work at the same company since graduation, leading to poor employee mobility. Most high-quality positions aren't filled through public hiring, making unemployment nearly fatal for Japanese workers. For a middle-aged unemployed man like Ryuhei, starting over is an extreme luxury. Ryuhei wandered the streets in a daze. As he turned a corner, a truck rushed toward him, knocking him to the ground. The driver panicked and fled the scene immediately, leaving Ryuhei unconscious on the roadside, his fate uncertain.

It wasn't just the couple; even their young son, Kenji, desperately wanted to escape the family. He snuck onto a long-distance bus, only to be caught by the driver for not having a ticket. When the police asked about his family address, he stubbornly refused to say. Helpless, the police had no choice but to place him in custody for the time being. And so, the family spent a grueling night in different places. The first to return home the next day was Kenji. The police didn't give him a hard time; after teaching him a lesson, they let him go. Then, Ryuhei's wife woke up in the beach cabin, but she couldn't find any sign of the robber. When she walked to the beach, she found the car was gone. 2 tire tracks on the ground led straight into the sea; the robber had committed suicide by driving into the ocean. The wife returned home to find her son waiting for her. On the other side, Ryuhei also woke up from his coma; he had been lying on the road all night. He took the money the robber had dropped to the lost and found, then slowly made his way home, still wearing his janitor's uniform.

The family sat down to eat as they always did, as if nothing had ever happened. Having experienced such a turbulent night, they finally understood that no matter what happens, life must go on. The TV was broadcasting the latest news on the Iraq War, reporting that the war was nearing its end and 3,000 international soldiers would soon return home. In the letter Takashi sent home, he admitted his mistakes to his parents, having once believed that America represented justice, only to realize now that to America, he was merely expendable cannon fodder, and that protecting his family was far more meaningful. And so, the 4 of them, who had all wanted to escape this home, reunited once again, ultimately choosing to compromise with life.

In the blink of an eye, 4 months passed. Ryuhei was still working as a cleaner at the mall, yet he dressed up to attend his son's piano exam. It was the first time he had heard his son play, and he never imagined his son was so gifted. The exam hall was packed, and even teachers from the next room stopped to listen.
