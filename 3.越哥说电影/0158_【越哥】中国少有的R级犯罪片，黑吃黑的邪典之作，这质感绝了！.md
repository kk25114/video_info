## 简介

一起装甲运钞车劫案引出连环血案：劫匪抢走车内枪支，内鬼与银行职员合谋抢劫信用社，警方循线追查，犯罪团伙却因分赃和灭口不断内讧。白卫、徐世海、黄秋叶与张虎等人相继卷入追杀，刑警杨志刚在多方线索中逼近真相。故事以枪战、复仇和逃亡收束，主要人物命运未定，留下续集悬念。

The armored truck driver was suddenly pulled over by a traffic cop. He was surprised, as this road was rarely used, and police were almost never seen there. He got out to ask what was going on, but the officer immediately demanded they open the safe in the back, which only deepened the pair's suspicions.

"Open it."

"Open it for us."

The officer said nothing, merely waving toward the distance, and another police car drove up. However, it wasn't the police who stepped out, but a gang of thugs. They had planned this for days, all to hijack this armored vehicle belonging to a mine owner. The 2 drivers were instantly subdued. The robber, Bai Wei, didn't hesitate, taking out prepared explosives and fixing them to the safe door. He pulled the fuse, and with a deafening blast, the safe door was blown wide open. In the chaos, 1 of the guards tried to run away. The other robbers, naturally not wanting to leave a loose end, immediately gave chase. When they dragged him back, they found Bai Wei had already stabbed the other driver to death. His reason was simple: he wanted the watch on the driver's wrist. The leader, Xu Shihai, was furious. Their original plan was just robbery; they never intended to take lives. But seeing a truck approaching in the distance, they had to finish what they started, ordering an accomplice to silence the remaining driver before fleeing the scene with the loot.

After the incident, police rushed to the scene. Criminal Investigation Captain Yang Zhigang, who was on vacation, received an emergency notice and had to cut his leave short to head to the front lines. As a forensic expert, Captain Yang carefully examined the scene and quickly made an initial assessment.

"Based on the footprints, there were 4 robbers. 2 of them were around 1.80 meters tall; their weights were 75 kilograms and 90 kilograms. One knew how to handle explosives, and one was limping."

He concluded the robbers wouldn't dare take the main road and would likely flee north along remote paths, so he immediately deployed officers to track them down. However, one thing baffled him: for such a violent robbery, the mine manager and vehicle supervisor insisted in unison that there were no valuables in the truck.

"Our mine's policy is to pay wages on the 5th of every month, no exceptions. Today is only the 3rd. So it was still empty; nothing was lost."

The captain didn't believe a word of it. After all, no stupid thief would kill 2 people for an empty truck; clearly, the cargo was the reason they were so desperate.

On the other side, the robbers' car broke down midway, forcing the group to abandon it and flee on foot, hungry and thirsty in the wilderness. Exhausted and freezing, the 4 of them traveled a long distance before finally spotting a house. The kind-hearted elderly woman living alone saw how miserable they were and warmly invited them inside, treating them like her own children. The robbers, their hands stained with blood, felt a rare stirring in their hearts. After eating and drinking their fill, the men took out money to compensate the woman. She initially refused to take it, but after some insistence, she reluctantly accepted. As the robbers prepared to continue their journey, the woman, worried they might go hungry on the road, gave them the food from her house.

Bai Wei turned and left without expression. The other 3 said nothing, but in his heart, Xu Shihai had already secretly decided to kill this accomplice who kept causing trouble.

Elsewhere, the police discovered the abandoned fake police car in the wilderness. Captain Yang immediately led his team to follow the leads, but they were still a step behind. With only the elderly woman's body left at the scene, Yang ordered a search of the nearby villages, and it turned out exactly as he had expected.

That evening, the 4 robbers arrived at the nearby Jida Town, where Xu Shihai's mistress, Huang Qiuye, was already waiting at the agreed-upon spot. Confirming the heist was successful, Huang Qiuye went to a private room at a restaurant to meet Zhang Zhipeng, the insider from the mine and the person in charge of the transport vehicle. The hush money was originally agreed at 10,000 yuan, but now that lives had been lost, the other party demanded a price hike on the spot.

"2 lives. 2 lives for just 10,000? I don't care how much more it is."

Huang Qiuye actually didn't know that lives had been lost during this operation. She immediately returned to the car to confirm the situation with Xu Shihai. Xu Shihai knew that Zhang Zhipeng could no longer be trusted and might report him to the police at any time, so the safest way to handle it was to silence him. As soon as Zhang Zhipeng left the restaurant, Xu Shihai followed him quietly. Sensing danger, Zhang Zhipeng hurriedly took out his phone to call the police, but was stabbed in the abdomen by Xu Shihai, who rushed out from the darkness. Seeing a passerby nearby, Xu Shihai didn't dare to linger; he snatched the money from Zhang Zhipeng's arms and fled in a hurry. His negligence once again left clues for the police. Severely injured, Zhang Zhipeng used his last bit of strength to call the Public Security Bureau Chief. Although there was no sound on the other end, the Chief deduced that he had likely been attacked and immediately notified Captain Yang to rush to the scene.

Meanwhile, Xu Shihai had reunited with the other 3 accomplices and returned to their hideout. Xu Shihai's gaze toward Bai Wei grew increasingly cold. Zhou Bing, standing by, saw through his boss's intentions; Bai Wei had broken the rules 2 times, and Xu Shihai now wanted to get rid of this thorn in his side. Zhou Bing walked to the window to keep watch, while Er Kui grabbed a bottle and smashed it against Bai Wei. Xu Shihai then took out a plastic bag and covered Bai Wei's head. With the 2 working together, Bai Wei soon stopped moving. Just then, Zhou Bing, who was on lookout, spotted the police approaching, and they quickly turned off the bedroom lights. Er Kui tried to stay calm as he opened the door. He knew the local officers well and hoped to bluff his way through. However, Captain Yang noticed the bowls and chopsticks on the table, realizing someone else might be there. Just as he was about to check the bedroom, news arrived that Zhang Zhipeng had been found, allowing the bandits to narrowly escape detection. Before they could even breathe a sigh of relief, Er Kui noticed that Bai Wei, who had been lying on the floor, was gone. He must have slipped out the window while they weren't looking. With no time to think, the 3 had to relocate.

Meanwhile, Captain Yang followed the clues and tracked them to Bai Wei. He used to be a blaster at a mine. His mother needed surgery, and he was desperate for cash. Plus, the mine had recently reported missing detonators. These traits matched the profile, so the police immediately named Bai Wei as the prime suspect. What Captain Yang didn't expect was that instead of hiding, Bai Wei took the offensive. He secretly found the mine owner and revealed his identity as a robber, threatening him with the knowledge of what was really in the armored truck.

"Give me 100,000, and I'll make this go away."

"How will you handle it?"

"Don't worry about that; I have my ways."

Finally, the boss agreed to pay the 100,000, but sent his own men to follow him to make sure he didn't run off with the money.

While Captain Yang was busy searching for the robbers, the police received a tip-off about 3 suspects matching the description preparing to flee the country from Qianmen Town. The police wouldn't miss such a chance and rushed to Qianmen Town. Little did they know, the call was a diversion by Huang Qiuye to draw the police away, unintentionally dragging someone else into the case. Zhang Hu was a fugitive drug dealer planning to cross the border from Qianmen Town, but because of Huang Qiuye's tip, he ended up running straight into the investigating team led by Captain Yang. Captain Yang was entirely focused on the armored truck robbery, so even though he suspected Zhang Hu was trouble, he didn't press him for details.

Just then, the station chief called. It turned out the severely injured Zhang Zhipeng had regained consciousness and provided a crucial clue: the items stolen from the truck were the factory director's lost weapons—2 Type 54 pistols and 1 Type 56 rifle. Captain Yang suddenly realized the truth: the gang's real target was never the money in the truck. They intended to use the stolen weapons to rob the credit union. In fact, Huang Qiuye was an employee of the credit union, and she planned this robbery with Xu Shihai and Li Yingwai. After diverting the police's attention with a fake alarm, they took immediate action; 3 gunmen stormed the bank and completed the heist in 10 minutes, getting away with 5 million in cash. The 3 prepared to take a back road to meet up with Huang Qiuye, but the police had anticipated their move and intercepted them on the way, leading to a fierce gunfight. Zhou Bing tried to sneak away with 2 bags of money but was shot dead on the spot; Xu Shihai and Er Kui managed to break through in a car, and despite the police chasing them wildly, the 2 eventually escaped. That night, they finally met up with Huang Qiuye, and the 3 hid in a hotel. Severely wounded, Er Kui knew he wouldn't last long and decided to leave the stolen money for his wife; Xu Shihai and Huang Qiuye did not stop him. However, they didn't know that Bai Wei, having escaped death, had already laid an ambush in Er Kui's home, and Er Kui was stabbed viciously as soon as he entered. Completely unable to fight back, Er Kui could only watch as Bai Wei assaulted his wife, and in the end, Bai Wei strapped explosives to them and blew the couple to death.

On the other side, Zhang Hu, who had planned to smuggle himself out, couldn't leave on time due to a police raid; suspecting Tongue had betrayed him, he arrived with a knife, demanding an explanation.

"The incident with Xiaowu was just an accident."

"Calm down."

"Don't worry, little brother; I've already handled your friend."

Because of this explanation, Tongue was finally able to calm Zhang Hu down. After Zhang Hu left, Huang Qiuye called; it turned out she also wanted to rely on Tongue to get abroad. Tongue demanded 200,000 and told her to meet at the agreed location at 3 p.m. tomorrow to arrange for them to escape with Zhang Hu.

Late at night, Xu Shihai and Huang Qiuye were fast asleep in the hotel; Bai Wei and his thugs crept over and forced the hotel owner to open a door, but they accidentally barged into the wrong room. The screams of the neighbor alerted Xu Shihai, who pulled a thug into the room the moment the door opened; in the dark, Xu Shihai fought back, pinning the man down, and shouted for Huang Qiuye to take the money and run. Although Xu Shihai was a formidable fighter, he was outnumbered, and under the siege of Bai Wei and his thugs, he was quickly suppressed. At the critical moment, Huang Qiuye grabbed a pair of scissors from the table and stabbed them hard into Bai Wei's arm, which gave Xu Shihai a chance to recover. He first finished off the thug with a knife, then started a brawl with Bai Wei, who had broken through the door. Bai Wei violently slammed Xu Shihai's head onto the nail-studded table. The intense pain and heavy impact knocked Xu Shihai into a daze. Bai Wei turned to pursue Huang Qiuye, but he was 1 step too late; Huang Qiuye had already driven far away. Deeply unwilling, Bai Wei prepared to return to the motel to finish off Xu Shihai, but he found that the police had already arrived, forcing him to give up. Isolated and helpless, Xu Shihai could only take the motel owner hostage, still deluding himself that he could escape. However, Captain Yang would not give him another chance; he fired decisively and killed Xu Shihai on the spot.

Having narrowly survived, Huang Qiuye didn't flee immediately; instead, she took the money and sought out Tongue.

"I'm not leaving. Help me kill someone. This is half the money. There's more after it's done."

Seeing the large bag of cash, Tongue was tempted. He already had a plan in mind to kill 2 birds with 1 stone, so he immediately agreed. That afternoon, Zhang Hu met with Tongue as planned to prepare for his escape, but Tongue suddenly pulled out a wanted poster of Zhang Hu. He implied that Zhang Hu hadn't paid enough; to continue with the escape, Zhang Hu had to kill someone for him, or Tongue would report him to the police. Zhang Hu had no choice but to bite the bullet and agree, and the person he had to kill was Bai Wei. Tongue told him to bring back Bai Wei's ear, the one with the earring, to complete the job.

At this point, Bai Wei had already returned to his hometown. As soon as he entered the door, he saw that Captain Yang had been waiting for a long time. He instinctively looked toward the grenade hidden in his bag, ready to take the police present down with him. However, a single sentence from his old mother made him instantly give up resistance: "Son, when you go to work, you must listen to your leader." Ultimately, he chose to cooperate with the police. It was only after they were completely out of his mother's sight that Captain Yang put the handcuffs on him. Just when it seemed everything had settled, Zhang Hu suddenly drove out from the side, slamming directly into the police car and flipping it over. Zhang Hu seized the chance to grab Bai Wei, but Captain Yang, despite his severe injuries, kept his gun trained on him. Zhang Hu had to use Bai Wei as a shield, and a standoff ensued. Knowing that being caught meant death, Zhang Hu stabbed Bai Wei in the neck on the spot. Captain Yang shot Zhang Hu in the shoulder, but then collapsed from his own critical injuries. Afterward, Zhang Hu cut off Bai Wei's ear and fled on foot along the highway. He escaped, but eventually collapsed on the ground due to excessive blood loss. Fortunately, a passing old man kindly rescued him and brought him home for treatment. The old man bandaged his wounds and draped a coat over him. This rare kindness from a stranger touched the heart of this ruthless killer. Zhang Hu took out a stack of cash, left it behind, and ignoring any protests, dragged his severely wounded body away alone.

Zhang Hu found Tongue and produced Bai Wei's ear, but Tongue was no pushover; he immediately called out several thugs in the house, planning to kill Zhang Hu. He clearly underestimated Zhang Hu's ruthlessness. Zhang Hu retreated to the door without a word, locked the iron gate himself, drew his signature weapon from his bag, and began a massacre. These green thugs were no match for him. Even while severely wounded, Zhang Hu's desperate, death-defying drive allowed him to slaughter all of the thugs. However, he forgot about Tongue, who was hiding in the shadows, and was ambushed, suffering a deep stab wound to the abdomen. At the critical moment, Tongue's wife, who had suffered years of domestic abuse, chose to turn against her husband and saved Zhang Hu. The woman's expression was calm, as if she were accustomed to such scenes. She coolly cleaned up the crime scene, then helped Zhang Hu vanish into the vast night. In the end, with the woman's help and an intermediary's arrangements, Zhang Hu fled toward the border, accompanied unexpectedly by Huang Qiuye. The 2 storylines converged once again, leaving suspense for the next part of the story. As for the fate of the 2 fugitives, we must wait for the director to reveal it.
