## 简介

视频文稿讲述了北宋时期范仲淹在杭州饥荒期间的非凡治理策略。面对百年一遇的饥荒，范仲淹不仅巧妙地通过政府干预平抑了粮价，还通过鼓励建筑、组织文化活动等手段促进了经济复苏和社会稳定。此外，文稿还回顾了范仲淹的政治生涯，包括他敢于直谏、不畏权贵的性格，以及他在军事上的卓越成就，特别是在对抗西夏的战争中展现出的战略眼光。范仲淹的故事展现了一个多才多艺、正直无私的历史人物形象，他的治理哲学和人格魅力对后世产生了深远影响。

In **1049**,  

There was a famine in Hangzhou, the worst in a hundred years.  
Many people starved to death.  
It was a terrible sight.  

However, the Liang merchants took the opportunity to elevate the rice price.  
The famine was exacerbated.  
All the people hoped that the government could open the granaries to relieve the disaster.  

However,  
Not only did the new officials not distribute a grain of food,  
They also thought food prices were too low,  
Encouraging grain merchants to continue raising prices.  
The price of rice doubled.  

For a moment, the grain merchants applauded,  
Moved to the "Blue Sky Big Master."  
The temple was closed.  

Foreign grain traders heard that Hangzhou rice prices rose,  
And all the rice was shipped to Hangzhou overnight,  
Ready to make a killing.  

For a time, the whole city of Hangzhou's waterway traffic was congested,  
Filled with caravans carrying rice.  

When the city of Hangzhou had a mountain of rice,  
The new governor suddenly ordered  
The distribution of all the grain in the government granary.  
With free rice to eat,  
Who would buy expensive rice?  

All of a sudden,  
The grain merchants who were preparing to make a fortune  
Were all dumbfounded.  
Rice wouldn't sell,  
And it cost more to ship back home.  
They had to sell at a reduced price.  
It went back and forth,  
And the price of rice in Hangzhou suddenly hit rock bottom.  

However,  
"A new broom sweeps clean"—  
Is that the end?  
It doesn't.  

The governor persuaded local couples,  
Saying labor costs were high in previous years.  
"Let's build more houses while labor is cheap,  
And then when times are better,  
Put the houses on the market.  
Bottom-fishing opportunities are rare—  
Don't miss it."  

The governor himself took the lead in hiring workers,  
Expanding his own yamen.  
Suddenly, tens of thousands of people had jobs to earn a living.  

Then he began to organize dragon boat races,  
Bringing a lot of fun.  
"Eat, drink, and be merry,"  
Attracting outsiders to visit Hangzhou.  

Soon, the city was full of small vendors  
And visitors to Hangzhou.  
The hotels were fully booked,  
A picture of prosperity and recovery.  

The divine manipulation of the governor  
Left the Northern Song court dumbstruck.  
Bidding up the price of rice,  
Promoting construction,  
"Eat, drink, and be merry"—  
In the year of the apocalypse,  
These would have been seen as corrupt actions,  
Yet they quickly solved the problem of famine.  

The success helped the people through the difficulties.  
I'm just asking you—isn't this good?  

And this man of God who went against the *Tao Te Ching*,  
Who worried about the world's worries before enjoying its joys,  
The Northern Song's first perfect man—  
**Fan Zhongyan**.  

Why is he the first perfect man?  
Fan Zhongyan was not only a writer,  
Thinker, and strategist,  
But also a politician and educator.  
A calligrapher to boot—a perfect "hexagonal warrior."  

But the most impressive thing about Teacher Fan  
Was not his political or military achievements,  
Though they were brilliant.  
It was his selflessness,  
His steadfast spirit of justice,  
Putting the queen over the fire,  
Allowing the emperor to "eat grass,"  
Daring to force the Lord of Western Xia to "eat dirt."  
Handsome? Yes.  
That's the kind of man he was.  

Let's start with him "holding the queen over the fire."  
Fan Zhongyan's father died when he was **2** years old.  
His mother remarried into the Zhu family.  
He studied hard as a teenager  
And passed the Jinshi examination at the age of **26**.  
Starting from grassroots work,  
Comrade Fan Zhongyan worked hard in those years,  
Unselfish,  
Receiving five-star reviews everywhere he went.  

Later, during his mother's death,  
He was hired back as a teacher by his alma mater.  
Because Teacher Fan's character was impeccable,  
His learning rigorous,  
He not only had "peaches and plums all over the world,"  
But was also recommended to the central government to advise the emperor.  

When Teacher Fan first came to Beijing as an official,  
The Northern Song court was not ruled by the emperor.  
At that time, Empress Dowager Liu E held court,  
Declaring edicts with one hand covering the sky.  
Emperor Renzong had ascended the throne at the age of **12**,  
Was about to enter graduate school age,  
And was just a mascot.  

What's worse,  
Liu E was also a thief,  
Loving cosplay,  
Always wearing imperial robes,  
Donning a crown,  
And having the emperor perform sacrificial activities.  
Yuck—Song Renzong!  

The emperor had come of age.  
It was high time for him to govern.  
The political ministers of the court were unhappy,  
But no one dared to break this window.  
After all, everyone knew—  
Until Fan Zhongyan came along.  

On the winter solstice of that year,  
Song Renzong prepared to lead civil and military officials  
To bow to Empress Dowager Liu in the Hall of Huiqing.  
After hearing this, Teacher Fan felt it was utterly inappropriate.  
"Emperor of the Great Song, you are the true dragon, the Son of Heaven.  
In private, out of filial piety,  
You can kneel all you want.  
But for official occasions, the temple is high.  
Doing this would demean the majesty of the Son of Heaven."  

He wrote a letter to Empress Dowager Liu,  
Asking her to retire gracefully  
And return power to the emperor at once.  
"The emperor is already in his early twenties.  
It's unethical to keep him here."  
He wrote another letter to Song Renzong,  
Urging him to stand tall  
And not do such a humiliating thing.  

This wave of operations directly angered Liu E,  
And he was banished from the capital at once.  

Actually, the emperor had come of age.  
It was natural for the empress dowager to retire.  
But as long as no one spoke up,  
Liu E could continue her rare act,  
Running the show in confusion.  
But Teacher Fan brought it up.  
The only place to roast the queen was over a fire.  
Not returning the government seemed too shameless.  
For a moment, Liu E was caught in a dilemma.  

But this empress dowager of the Northern Song Dynasty was comparable to Lü Wu—  
A tough woman.  
She did not give power to the emperor until her death.  

Logically, after this education,  
Teacher Fan should have learned tact.  
But he was not one to avoid trouble.  
He never learned tact in his life.  
As recorded in the *Song Dynasty*,  
Fan Zhongyan was "not grateful about the world, regardless of oneself."  
When it came to matters of the world,  
He was a man who didn't care about life or death.  
How could he overshadow the light of justice?  

When Song Renzong finally survived Liu E,  
He soon learned Teacher Fan's honesty.  
After Liu E's death,  
Song Renzong finally breathed a sigh of relief.  
"After all, she wasn't my birth mother.  
I've been holding myself back for years."  
The grievance in Baby Renzong's heart was great.  

After taking power,  
He spoke up for Fan Zhongyan  
And recalled him to the capital.  
On the other hand,  
He began purging the old queen's party.  

Speaking of Empress Dowager Liu E,  
That's a legend too.  
She started in hell mode:  
Both parents died when she was a baby,  
Fostered in her mother's Ningzhou,  
Grew up a little,  
Became a singing girl,  
Made a living by busking.  

At **15**, she married a silversmith in Shu.  
Later, the silversmith took Liu E to Bianjing for a living.  
When they were too poor to survive,  
He sold her.  
But this wave of operations  
Directly let Liu E fly to the branches as a phoenix.  

The buyer of Liu E  
Was Zhao Heng, the later Emperor Zhenzong of the Song Dynasty.  
His men presented Liu E to Zhao Heng.  
Zhao Heng saw Liu E,  
Immediately had a nosebleed,  
And his soul was taken away.  
He had a lifelong crush on her,  
Never fading.  

How much did Zhao Heng dote on Liu E?  
Liu E was abandoned by her ex-husband,  
But she still thought about him.  
Then, when she became successful,  
She made her ex-husband a brother.  
Zhao Heng, the incumbent, was not at all averse,  
Had a lot to say about her ex-husband,  
And promoted him to an official position.  

After Liu E became empress dowager,  
She was even more of a "wife."  
During her reign,  
Ministers submitted memorials requesting Liu E to "face the noon sun,"  
And the Duke of Wei offered Liu E the "facing the sun" ritual.  
This pile went on and on.  

Those were the things that hurt Baby Renzong's heart.  
But after he came to power,  
Not only did he take care of the empress dowager's party,  
He even denied any connection with the queen,  
Sick to death of it.  
All the civil and military ministers followed suit,  
Vigorously carrying out loyalty activities for the queen mother.  

But at this point,  
Teacher Fan spoke up again.  
He advised Song Renzong:  
"The queen mother did a lot of good.  
No credit, no toil, no fatigue—  
No more grumbling.  
If you act like this,  
How unfilial will people see you?"  
Is this political height still necessary?  
The other side's "wall grass evil wind"  
Must also be contained.  

Actually, filial piety is another matter.  
The key question is this:  
Liu E was the de facto ruler of the Northern Song Dynasty for **11** years.  
Although there were some disadvantages,  
Overall, she was a more effective female politician.  
Some of her policies  
And reforms were commendable.  
If you deny all her previous work because you hate Liu E,  
That would certainly be unwise.  

Fan Zhongyan was impartial,  
Rational and objective.  
So he could always see things as they were,  
Insight into the underlying logic of the problem.  
Thus,  
His righteous words were not simple bloodlust,  
But about not being governed by emotions,  
Not disturbed by others,  
The courage and determination to uphold the truth.  

This is unlike many single-minded ministers in history,  
Too busy fighting up there.  
It's very different.  

Two examples illustrate the point:  
When Fan Zhongyan had not yet entered the exams,  
He was studying at the academy.  
One day, Song Zhenzong, the great boss, came to Yingtianfu for an inspection.  
All the students ran to see the fun—  
"An emperor's words are like gold!"  
Only Fan Zhongyan was unmoved.  
Some students were curious,  
Asking why he wasn't going.  
"I suspect he's either too old or too young—  
The spots."  
But Fan Zhongyan said:  
"The book reads well—sooner or later, I can see the emperor.  
It's hard to see what's useful now."  

I have to say,  
He's the kind of guy who only gets to the point,  
An attitude that only looks at the underlying logic.  
It's destined to do something big.  

And when his mother died,  
A burial plot was chosen.  
Feng shui said this cemetery was a "death trap"—  
Burying your mother there would kill your children.  
Fan Zhongyan, however, said:  
"If it's a death trap,  
Then let my family take care of it.  
If it's my destiny to die,  
That's not gonna help."  
So he insisted on burying his mother there.  
Fan Zhongyan later had four sons,  
And his descendants flourished.  
The rumor fell apart.  

It follows that  
"A wise man has no doubts"—  
Rational and objective,  
This is the background color of Fan Zhongyan's impartiality.  

But also because of his integrity,  
Right and wrong,  
Telling the truth too much,  
He repeatedly faced exclusion.  
Even the baby emperor loved and hated him,  
Banishing him for years and bringing him back,  
Recalling and dismissing him.  
The central and local governments repeatedly made false choices.  

Song Renzong was no bad king.  
He also knew that Teacher Fan was a selfless loyal minister.  
But some things did make him look bad,  
So he had to disparage him.  

In the second year of Mingdao,  
Locusts swarmed the Jianghuai region.  
The victims were running everywhere.  
Teacher Fan, worried about the country and the people,  
Wrote a letter to the emperor,  
Urging him to send someone for disaster relief.  
Emperor Renzong indulged in harem beauties,  
Singing and dancing all day,  
Totally out of the question.  

Later, Fan Zhongyan urged again and again.  
Tired of being called,  
The emperor said:  
"Since you're worried about it,  
Then I'll send you to take care of it."  

Fan Zhongyan arrived in the disaster area,  
Opened the granaries to relieve the disaster,  
Exempted taxes,  
Mobilized the people to help themselves.  
The disaster was soon under control,  
And the people got through the hard times.  
It was a great achievement.  

Renzong was happy too.  
But when Teacher Fan returned to the court,  
He went straight to Renzong,  
Almost dying of rage.  
He brought back to Renzong a bundle of weeds,  
Telling Song Renzong:  
"Before the victims had no food to eat,  
They just ate these weeds."  
He let Song Renzong  
Be sure to taste the weed,  
Emphasizing that not only the emperor should eat it,  
But especially the concubines in the harem.  

Maybe today we don't think that's too much,  
But in ancient times, court discussions were matters of state.  
Little was said of the harem's concubines—  
After all, it belonged to the emperor's private life.  
And Fan Zhongyan saying this  
Was clearly a satire on Emperor Renzong's indulgence in beauty.  

Song Renzong was furious,  
Thinking Fan Zhongyan meddled too much,  
And banished him from the capital.  

Fan Zhongyan later served in Muzhou and Suzhou,  
Where local people's livelihoods prospered.  
Renzong missed him again  
And called him back once more.  
So Teacher Fan was dismissed and recalled,  
The back and forth becoming routine.  

I have to say,  
Gold shines wherever it goes.  
And not only in governance—  
Fan Zhongyan was even more impressive in the military field.  
"The most powerful man in the world,"  
Forcing the Tangut king to "eat dirt"—  
What the hell is going on?  

In **1038**, the first year of Baoyuan in the Northern Song Dynasty,  
Western Xia's Li Yuanhao declared himself emperor,  
Breaking the vassal relationship with the Song Dynasty.  
He also defeated the Song army at the Battle of Sanchuankou,  
Rubbing the Song's face on the ground.  

At that time, the Song Dynasty had gone through three dynasties,  
Was in its fourth season.  
They lost battles with Liao,  
Lost games against Tibet,  
Now even the former Western Xia provincial team couldn't win.  
Renzong's heart was even more shameless.  
"The national team won't win—  
Time to popularize geography to the whole country."  

So Fan Zhongyan took office again,  
Sent to the northwest to deal with the Tanguts.  
He and his best friend Han Qi  
Served as two deputy commanders,  
One garrisoning Yanzhou (today's Yan'an),  
The other guarding the southern part of Ningxia.  
They were the main points of Western Xia's southern relatives.  

It's just that the relationship was strong,  
But strategic views diverged sharply.  
Cooperation was not as tacit as imagined.  
Han Qi believed that although Western Xia was rampant,  
It was still a small country,  
With all armies combined less than **100,000**.  
"We have vast land and abundant resources—  
We should be proactive,  
Just do it,  
Take out the main force,  
Put out the fire."  

Fan Zhongyan's view was different.  
He was not blinded by GDP at face value.  
Calmly and objectively analyzing the underlying logic,  
He believed they couldn't take the initiative.  
Instead, they should use gestures.  

"Who is to kill the domestic? Of course, it's cool—  
Everybody wants to, but it's not that simple.  
First, Western Xia was founded in Yinchuan, Ningxia,  
With bad weather,  
High mountains and far roads,  
Extremely difficult terrain.  
If the Song army goes deep into its hinterland,  
It's hard to find their main force,  
And extremely easy to ambush.  
They could eventually be wiped out—  
Like the rhythm of an imperial graveyard.  

Second, the Song army was not effective.  
After a long period of prosperity,  
Many soldiers had never seen the field,  
Lacked good martial skills,  
And couldn't conduct deep attacks.  

On the contrary, Western Xia  
Was mainly cavalry,  
With tough infantry  
And very good at ambush and mobile warfare.  
At the Battle of Sanchuankou,  
The Song army was made into dumplings by the Western Xia cavalry.  

In addition,  
Although Western Xia was small,  
Its military skills were not backward—  
On the same level as the Song Dynasty.  

The most important point was the poor mass base at the border.  
Whether Qiang or Han,  
Because the war continued for years,  
Life had been very close to death,  
And they couldn't help the Song army  
Launch a massive military operation."  

So Fan Zhongyan's strategy was:  
First, avoid lightning in many places in Yan'an,  
Strengthen fortifications.  
Second, launch a mass production campaign—  
"Do it yourself,  
Plenty of food and clothing."  
Third, launch a national united front—  
"Civil war,  
National training."
