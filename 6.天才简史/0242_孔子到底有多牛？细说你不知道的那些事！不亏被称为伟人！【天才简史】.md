## 简介

这段视频文稿详细介绍了中国古代伟大的思想家、教育家孔子的生平、教育理念及其对中华文化和世界文明的深远影响。从孔子的出生、成长、求学、教育实践到政治生涯，再到晚年的流浪和最终的逝世，文稿全面展现了孔子如何在春秋战国时期的动荡社会中，通过创立儒家学说和开办私塾，提倡‘有教无类’的教育平等理念，培养了大批弟子，影响了无数后人。孔子的思想不仅在中国历史上占据重要地位，也对世界文化产生了广泛而深远的影响。

He was holding a roll of bamboo slips,  
I'm covered in classic learning  
From more than 2,000 years ago.  

Don't ask for glitz,  
Don't chase fame and fortune.  
For the purpose of preaching and enlightening,  
He founded a school of thought,  
Created a new education concept.  
It has affected the Chinese nation  
And the world as a whole.  

He is the new Master of Wisdom,  
A symbol of the great monument of Chinese civilization—Confucius.  

**A Brief History of Genius**  
Take you to the familiar and unfamiliar Sage: Confucius.  

**Late Spring and Autumn Period**  
Royal decline,  
The princes contended for power.  
The war on eroticism continues,  
Kill for power,  
Dispute of suspicion.  
The wolf's ambition in all directions  
Made for an era of bad moon collapse.  

And it's in a place like this,  
In the midst of all this chaos,  
Confucius was born.  

It is said that Confucius was born with a hollow top of his head,  
Strange eyebrows and eyes.  
And because he was born in the mountains of mud,  
Hence the name Qiu, styled Zhongni.  

Confucius was three years old  
When his father died.  
Then came the decline of the family.  
Yan Zheng, his mother, was banished from the house.  
In desperation,  
She returned home with Confucius.  

The life of the orphan and widowed mother was very poor.  
Confucius, as a child, made a living  
By doing a lot of rough work.  
He once said, "I have seen much,  
But despise little."  

Fortunately, behind the suffering,  
And the teachings of his mother and grandfather,  
Guiding him to become a filial,  
Wise, and virtuous gentleman.  

Confucius also lived up to expectations.  
He could always remember things very quickly.  
At a very young age, he was already comfortable teaching people  
And had his own understanding of governing the country.  

At the age of 15,  
He became more determined to learn,  
Devoting his life to learning  
And the pursuit of truth.  

That's how  
Confucius learned more and more,  
Became more and more knowledgeable.  
His claims grew higher.  
Even the emperor of the time, Duke Zhao of Lu, had heard about him.  
When his child was born,  
He sent a carp  
To give his congratulations.  

Disrespectful in one night,  
Taking the east wind,  
Confucius entered the official career smoothly.  
It was just a small position,  
But he still dutifully did his job.  

The way to receive  
The power of loyalty—  
But the gentleman does not abandon  
His ambition, which always lies in profound learning.  

For more than a decade,  
Confucius moved around  
Between various sesame-and-mung-bean-sized official posts.  
The pace of learning never stopped;  
He improved day by day,  
Cast in stone.  

He became highly skilled in the six traditional Confucian arts.  
His reputation gradually spread far and wide.  

He later gave up his official position  
And officially established a school,  
Began teaching,  
And cultivated talents.  

The life of Confucius  
Followed the policy of "no class distinction in education."  
He focused solely on learning,  
No matter how poor or wealthy the students were—  
They were all under the same roof.  

This idea of equality in education  
Had a meaningful impact  
On the development of later Chinese history.  
China's thousands of years of cultural heritage  
Have been passed down because of it.  

In terms of education,  
In addition to advocating equality for all,  
He also taught students according to their aptitude,  
Using rites and people to enlighten the disciples.  

For more than 40 years in education,  
His disciples numbered as many as 3,000.  
Among them,  
Zi Gong and others formed  
The famous "Seventy-Two Wise Men."  

After opening his lectures,  
Confucius was already a famous master,  
But still not because of the peach world.  
He was proud of himself on the guard,  
"There is no end to learning."  

He was determined to preach,  
Wanting to restore the Spring and Autumn Period's benevolence, righteousness, and courtesy.  
He sought knowledge from Lao Dan, asked Changhong,  
Learned the qin, recited poetry,  
Asked for the fish list,  
Discussed the hegemony of Duke Mu of Qin with Duke Jing of Qi,  
Consulted with the stepson Shao Yue about beauty's doubts.  

Confucius always consulted others for knowledge.  
It is also because of this spirit of inquiry  
That later,  
In moral aesthetics,  
Political economy,  
Education, history, and other aspects,  
He raised the mystery to great heights.  

In 501 BC,  
The 51-year-old Confucius  
Was appointed as Zhongdu Zai.  
After taking office,  
He stood firm,  
The wind blew all over the place.  
He practiced courtesy,  
Got rid of bad habits.  

Over the course of a year,  
He turned Zhongdu into a commercial boom,  
A land of simple folk and great harmony.  
Later,  
By virtue of his outstanding political record,  
He worked his way up from governor to senior official.  

Because of his crackdown on the powerful,  
Confucius was regarded by all powers as a thorn in their flesh.  
Meanwhile, the court of Lu began to degenerate and corrupt.  
Kings indulged in sensuality,  
Did not ask about politics.  

The career path was dark.  
A distressed Confucius was forced to flee his hometown.  
With a crowd of disciples,  
He began more than 14 years of traveling around the world.  

Life on the road was unexpectedly hard.  
War, hunger, doubt—  
Even death was once so close.  
Confucius, a famous teacher of his generation,  
Was once known as "the one who could die for righteousness."  

During that long time,  
He wandered with his disciples,  
Tossing and turning in Wei, Cao,  
Song, Zheng,  
Ye, Chu, and other countries,  
Talking to countless politicians,  
Describing his political ideals.  
But he was always frustrated—  
No country was willing to embrace his ideas.  

Full of ambition,  
In the drift from place to place,  
It eventually obliterated into emptiness.  

In 484 BC,  
Confucius finally got a chance to set foot in his native land,  
Back to the place he had been living for a long time.  
He was now in his seventies,  
Having long lost his passion for integrity.  
He had to put his hopes back in education.  

In order to better implement education,  
In his later years, he began to write books,  
Revised and sorted out the Six Classics,  
Leaving the last spiritual legacy for the teacher.  

In the last days of his life,  
Confucius experienced the loss of his wife and son,  
The death of his favorite student.  
A series of blows nearly wore him down in his old age.  

Before he died, he advocated:  
"Mount Tai is crumbling,  
The beam is broken,  
The philosopher is withering away."  

On April 11, 479 BC,  
The ancient philosopher Confucius passed away.  
He was 73 years old.  

A group of his disciples went on a pilgrimage,  
Recording the teacher's daily instructions in a book—  
*The Analects of Confucius*—  
To hand down to future generations.  

Since Emperor Wu of Han Dynasty,  
Confucianism began to dominate,  
Respecting Confucius' teachings,  
Which flourished for thousands of years  
Around the world,  
Having a profound impact.  

For Chinese culture,  
It is a shining star in the long night,  
An oasis suddenly emerging from the desert.  

Somebody said that  
In this world,  
There is a people who do not believe in God  
But believe in a man.  
By this faith,  
This troubled nation  
Became the world's first  
And the only people without a break in history.  

For more than 2,000 years,  
They have created a brilliant culture.  
This man was Confucius.  
This nation is the Chinese nation.  

In my mind,  
Confucius was concerned about his country and his people,  
Always unwavering.  
He had a kind face,  
Spoke with difficulty,  
Wobbled on his crutch.  

In the barbaric social context of the time,  
He was not well fed or clothed,  
Was bullied,  
Faced peril,  
And couldn't even think about it now—  
Or I might be crying from my sweat.  

But years later,  
When I got to know Confucius inside out,  
I realized how wrong I was.  
After all, in the chaotic Spring and Autumn Period,  
To be the leader of 3,000 students,  
Trying to get all the counties to respect you—  
All it took was a frail old man?  
But I can't.  

So today, we're going to talk about  
Why Confucius is known as the Sage,  
And how great he actually was.  

Confucius lived during the Spring and Autumn Period.  
What was that period's most famous feature?  
We all know the Zhou Dynasty was generous,  
Rewarding directly with fiefdoms.  

By the early Spring and Autumn Period,  
There were more than 140 vassal states.  
These countries were big and small,  
Like snakes trying to survive—  
They could only continue to devour weaker countries.  

In just over 200 years,  
There were as many as 483 wars recorded.  
If you do the math, it's probably more than 1,000.  
By the end of the Spring and Autumn Period,  
Only 14 or 15 of the original 140 countries remained.  

It is self-evident how chaotic this period was.  
Confucius, on the other hand, thrived in turbulent times.  
How much did people miss Confucius?  

First, let's look at his own state of Lu.  
Confucius was a very influential man in Lu.  
In his early years,  
He didn't leave much of an official mark, did he?  
But there's always a legend about him.  

Confucius had more than 3,000 disciples.  
Some were heads of scholar-official families in Lu,  
Some later served as officials in both Wei and Lu,  
Some were excellent military strategists  
Who commanded armies  
And defeated opponents stronger than their own country.  
Some made fortunes in business,  
Becoming as rich as a country.  

So to speak,  
At that time, all the high officials in Lu knew Confucius was a treasure,  
But no one dared to use him  
Because of political checks and balances.  
Anyone who used Confucius openly  
Would face sieges from other forces.  

Until a vassal named Yang Hu rebelled,  
Then everyone agreed  
To hire Confucius, who was 50 years old at the time.  

Less than two years after Confucius came to power,  
He rose from a minor official  
To the president of the Supreme Court.  
That's pretty much it.  

Of course,  
The State of Lu producing such a talent  
Meant other countries heard about it,  
Including the State of Qi,  
Which had a delicate relationship with Lu.  

The relationship between Duke Jing of Qi and Confucius is also hard to define.  
When Duke Jing of Qi first saw Confucius,  
Confucius was not yet in such a high position in Lu.  
But Duke Jing made a special visit,  
Speaking highly of Confucius.  

The second time they met was in Qi.  
This time, Duke Jing asked Confucius directly  
About "ruler-ruler, minister-minister, father-father, son-son."  
This made Duke Jing very happy.  
The more he saw Confucius, the more he liked him,  
Directly preparing to hire him.  
But he was stopped by his own advisors  
And had to give up.  

The next time they met,  
Confucius had become an important official of Lu,  
And their relationship grew even more antagonistic.  
At the famous meeting of Jia and Guo,  
The State of Qi wanted to attack the weaker State of Lu,  
Throwing insult after insult.  
But under Confucius' orders,  
Lu not only did not insult back  
But instead regained three pieces of land.  

The Confucius he should have recruited  
Now became his enemy.  
This made the State of Qi think:  
"He who gains Confucius gains the world.  
Since we can't get him,  
No other country can either."  

So the State of Qi broke ranks,  
Damaging the relationship between Confucius and the people of Lu.  
At last, Confucius was disheartened  
And had to leave his homeland.  

But Confucius, now in his 50s,  
Was still very popular in all countries.  
The Wei army made overtures to him,  
The rebels of Jin before their rebellion  
Prepared to hire him as a high-paid consultant,  
The Chu army offered a 700-li fiefdom—  
All they did was fight and kill.  

The county kings, whom even the Son of Heaven despised,  
Why did they like Confucius so much?  
At the end of the day, Confucius was just too charming.  

Confucius's origins were nothing special.  
His father was an aristocrat,  
But he died when Confucius was three.  
So Confucius lacked family support.  

At that time,  
An essential skill for aristocrats was ritual and music.  
Although Confucius' standard of living did not reach that of aristocrats,  
In these skills,  
He did the best he could.  

First was ritual.  
From a very young age,  
Confucius began setting up sacrificial rites,  
Practicing etiquette.  
Later, to learn more complete etiquette,  
He studied in the Imperial Temple of Lu  
And even went to other countries.  

Confucius' spirit of seeking knowledge  
Is reflected in his words:  
"Wei Nan Gong studied quietly,  
Nothing has been heard of Lao Dan till now—  
The source of courtesy and music,  
The rules of virtue—my teacher also."  

This is the end.  
The quiet book and the state of residence—  
Wanli in the old Dan,  
Visiting Yue and Changhong.  
"Old Dan" here refers to Laozi,  
Another famous thinker.  

After studying with Laozi,  
Confucius mastered ritual thoroughly,  
Becoming the most knowledgeable person in the world at that time.  

Some might not understand:  
Confucius worked so hard  
Just to be a polite person?  
And with a little courtesy, you can be a teacher?  

Let’s clarify:  
Confucius' "ritual" is not modern politeness  
But something deeper and larger.  

For example,  
The rite of passage for men in ancient times  
Was solemn and tedious.  
Through it,  
A child became an adult,  
Gaining more respect  
And greater responsibility.  

Many admonitions and blessings were given:  
"Don't slack off on your appearance,  
Take care of your inner virtue."  
Relatives were all present  
To witness the fulfillment of adult virtue:  
"May you live long and auspiciously,  
Multiply your blessings."  

Before the ceremony,  
Ancient people used divination to choose the day, time, and guest of honor.  
In addition, they needed to display banners,  
Prepare step-fu and clothes,  
Cast wine after the ceremony,  
Worship the mother, give a name—  
A series of procedures more complex than most weddings today.  

In Zhou rituals,  
Not only weddings and funerals had strict codes,  
But even food and daily life  
Followed systematic rules.  

In the Spring and Autumn Period, when rituals collapsed,  
Confucius sought to restore Zhou rituals  
Not because he valued formalism,  
But because behind Zhou rituals lay morality.  
Rituals were a way  
To review moral standards,  
The concrete manifestation of reason and virtue.  

*Historical Records* records:  
"Confucius ruled with ritual for only three months,  
And the State of Lu saw obvious changes:  
Merchants dared not raise prices at will,  
Men and women walked in separate lanes,  
Lost items on the road were not taken,  
All guests from afar felt at home."  

Thus, Confucius' rituals restrained individuals  
But also provided the foundation for social stability.  
This is why  
All monarchs held him in high regard.  

Of course, this is not the full picture of Confucius.  
Because he was so great,  
It's often overlooked that  
Confucius was actually a tough guy.  

The average height of men in the Spring and Autumn Period was 1.56 meters,  
But Confucius was 9 feet 6 inches tall—  
About 1.89 meters today.  
Imagine:  
When you think Confucius was a gentle scholar  
Persuading others with reason,  
What if someone tried to neutralize him by force?  

Aside from his height,  
Confucius's strength was terrifying.  
*Spring and Autumn Annals of Lu* records:  
"Confucius lifted the gate bolt with his bare hands."  
This strength was inherited from his father,  
One of Lu's three tiger generals,  
Who once held up a city gate  
To let trapped soldiers escape.  

This bolt weighed about 300–400 pounds.  
Such physical advantages  
Naturally added weight to Confucius' teachings.  
After all, no student would dare disobey  
A teacher who could kick their butt  
But chose gentle reasoning instead.  

But some of Confucius' students were tough too.  
Take Zi Lu:  
Before meeting Confucius,  
He was a famous street fighter,  
Wearing exaggerated hats  
And strutting with a sword.  
As a bully,  
He looked down on Confucius' bookishness  
And tried to intimidate him.  

The next day,  
Zi Lu returned in formal attire  
With a gift,  
And Confucius gained a new student.  

Aside from ritual and music,  
Confucius' martial skills stood out.  
In the movie *Confucius*,  
There’s a scene where Confucius draws his bow  
And pierces a thick city wall—  
A testament to his archery skills.  

As a marksman,  
Confucius ignored perched birds,  
Only targeting those in flight.  
When asked about his skills,  
He said:  
"My heart is in my mouth when I shoot,  
Striving for straightness."  

In archery and charioteering,  
Confucius considered himself better at the latter.  
Ancient charioteering required three men,  
With the driver responsible for crucial maneuvers—  
Avoiding obstacles and planning attacks.  
It combined skill and tactics.
