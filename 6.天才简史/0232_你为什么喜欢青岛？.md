## 简介

The video script presents Qingdao as a multifaceted city with a rich historical background and vibrant modern life. It highlights Qingdao's historical significance as part of the Dongyi culture, its agricultural and commercial roots dating back thousands of years, and its evolution into a modern economic and cultural hub. The script also emphasizes Qingdao's natural beauty, culinary delights, especially its famous beer, and its cultural festivals, including the Qingdao International Beer Festival. The city is portrayed as a blend of tradition and modernity, offering a unique lifestyle that combines scenic beauty, cultural richness, and economic vitality.

What is Qingdao?

Some people say that Qingdao is the capital of wealth,  
The pearl of the north,  
The gold coast,  
The economic center.  

Some say Qingdao is a fairyland on earth:  
Deep blue seas,  
Silver sandy beaches.  

Some say Qingdao is a cultural paradise:  
Green tiles and red walls,  
A blend of traditions,  
The island of music,  
The sailing capital,  
The film and television city.  

Yet more who have been to Qingdao will say  
Qingdao has been given too many labels.  
Its history, humanities,  
Art, fashion,  
Scenery, and food each have their own charm.  
It's hard to explain in just a word or two.  

Anyway, this is a place you'll come and never want to leave—  
A wonderful destination.  

So, what is the real Qingdao?  

In this episode, we travel through its past and present,  
Taking you through this ancient, mysterious,  
Yet vibrantly modern city,  
To appreciate its historical weight and contemporary charm.  

First, we travel back 7,000 years.  
On the southeastern shore of the Jiaodong Peninsula by the Yellow Sea,  
The ancestors of the Dongyi tribe settled this land,  
Thriving and unfolding a magnificent oceanic civilization.  
This legacy, as part of the splendid history of the Chinese nation,  
Left behind a rich and colorful Dongyi culture,  
Which forms the roots of Qingdao's historical civilization.  

2,500 years ago, during the Spring and Autumn Period,  
In the 15th year of Duke Ling of Qi (567 BC),  
Qi destroyed Lai and established the city of Jimo,  
The ancient predecessor of Qingdao.  
Before Jimo was built,  
This land belonged to the Tangyi of Laiyi,  
With a long tradition of wheat cultivation,  
Marking the origins of Qingdao's agriculture and animal husbandry.  

Mr. Wang Xiantang recorded in *The Origins of Shandong Grains*  
That Laiyi was the earliest cultivator of wheat in ancient China.  
The agricultural expertise of Jimo was advanced,  
With high grain yields.  
Qingdao's unique climatic conditions  
Also made it a natural treasure for farming and herding.  

Located on the southeastern coast of the Shandong Peninsula,  
Qingdao belongs to the northern temperate monsoon zone,  
Influenced by moist southeast winds from the ocean,  
With ample rainfall.  
Additionally, Qingdao is rich in solar resources,  
Averaging 2,550.7 hours of sunshine annually,  
With a sunlight percentage of 58%,  
Providing ideal conditions for crop growth.  

During the era of King Xuan of Qi,  
Mencius recorded that Qi suffered famine,  
And he persuaded the ruler to open Jimo's granaries  
To relieve the victims.  
This shows Jimo has long been the "Eastern Granary" of the nation.  

Beyond grain cultivation,  
Jimo's lush water and grasslands  
Supported thriving animal husbandry.  
For example, *The Book of Documents* and *Zuo Zhuan* record  
That during the Spring and Autumn Period,  
The Laiyi people raised fine cattle and horses,  
Even using them as bribes to deter Qi's army.  

Thanks to advanced farming and herding,  
Qi's sages left extensive breeding knowledge,  
Such as *The Six Arts of Qi* and *Fan Li's Classic of Fish Farming*.  
When Yan's army invaded Qi, destroying 72 cities,  
Tian Dan's eventual victory relied on Jimo's robust livestock industry—  
Where else could he have sourced a thousand cattle for his fire-ox strategy?  

In the Western Han Dynasty,  
Jimo native Xu Wan formulated and verified the 24 solar terms,  
A landmark contribution to Chinese agricultural civilization.  

Beyond agriculture,  
Jimo was also a hub for silk, salt, and iron—  
The roots of Qingdao's commerce.  
As early as the Xia Dynasty,  
Laiyi paid tribute with river crabs,  
Silk, and seafood, transported endlessly by boat.  
The "Jiaodong silk" recorded in ancient texts  
Remains a local specialty today.  

The Laiyi were also skilled in iron smelting.  
Archaeologists at the Longkou site unearthed  
High-quality iron and bronze ware,  
Inscribed with records of master craftsmen.  
This reflects advanced metallurgy at the time.  
Many local villages still bear names  
Linked to salt boiling and iron smelting.  

Historians like Fan Wenlan believe  
China's iron industry likely originated here.  
After Qi destroyed Lai,  
It rewarded meritorious officials with iron tools,  
Further boosting Jimo's role as a commercial center.  

The wealth of Qi stemmed from this.  
Legends like *The Money Maker* describe  
Merchants amassing fortunes through trade.  
Historical records name Jimo  
The second-largest city in Qi,  
The political, military, economic, and cultural heart of Jiaodong.  

Qi's economy led the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods,  
With its knife-shaped coins renowned nationwide.  
Though Qin later standardized currency,  
Qi's knife coins remained the finest of their era.  

For millennia,  
From the ancient port of Langya to Jiaozhou's markets,  
Qingdao's commercial legacy has thrived,  
Marking its historical trajectory.  

Beyond prosperity,  
Qingdao's beauty spans ages,  
Captivating visitors like a fairyland.  
Emperor Qin Shi Huang visited Langya Terrace thrice,  
So enchanted he built a palace there.  
Later, he sent Xu Fu to seek immortality from its shores.  

From ancient times to the Ming and Qing dynasties,  
Qingdao blended wealth and beauty,  
Reality and fantasy,  
A marvel for millennia.  

In modern times, its shipping industry rose,  
Transforming it into the "Gold Coast."  
With 49 bays and a 905.2-kilometer coastline,  
Its ports handle 630 million tons of cargo annually.  

As Shandong's largest economy,  
Qingdao's GDP consistently leads.  
Its robust industry and comprehensive strength  
Keep it among China's top 100 cities.  
Recently, as a Belt and Road hub,  
It gained national strategic status,  
Hosting the SCO Local Economic Trade Demo Zone.  

Modern commerce bred famous brands like Haier, Hisense, and Tsingtao Beer.  
This open, cultured city ranks among China's top 3 happiest.  

Qingdao's happiness includes:  
Scenic beauty, music, sailing, film culture,  
And especially its cuisine—  
Qingdao potstickers, spicy fish balls, mackerel dumplings,  
Oil-sautéed conch, ginger crab, and spicy clams  
Are must-tries, with potstickers earning national fame.  

But Qingdao's most famous culinary icon is beer.  
In summer, under sea breezes,  
Nothing beats cold beer with barbecue.  
Locals adore beer—men, women, even kids "grow up shooting beer."  
A unique sight is locals carrying beer in plastic bags,  
A tradition born from 1970s shortages.  

Fresh "bag beer," unpasteurized with active yeast,  
Is a one-day shelf-life delicacy only found here.  

Half of Qingdao is seawater, half beer.  
Summer highlights include sea baths—  
Swimming at golden beaches like No. 1 Bathing Beach.  
Another quirk? Locals chat about "seaweed washing" like socializing.  
Legends even say love sparks in Qingdao's seaside romances.  

The Qingdao International Beer Festival,  
Launched in 1991, is now Asia's largest,  
Rivaling Germany's Oktoberfest.  
The 2021 "Cloud Beer Festival" innovated online celebrations.  

Beyond drinking, the festival features performances,  
Drinking contests, photo exhibitions, seafood feasts,  
Bike stunts, opera, and martial arts—  
A feast for eyes and palate.  

In 2017, Qingdao's West Coast District launched  
"Film City," "Music City," "Beer City," and "Expo City" initiatives,  
Boosting cultural soft power for economic growth.  

From July 22–31 this year,  
The 32nd Qingdao International Beer Festival returns,  
Inviting global beer lovers to a "Cloud Carnival."  
Follow updates for surprises!  

The Qingdao Beer Festival—  
The most beautiful West Coast awaits you!
